# Style

How videos on this channel are edited. Every agent reads this before it edits.
A rule is added only after the owner says yes. Each rule says where it came from.

## Every video
- Phones first: most viewers watch on a phone, desktop is rare. Every text on screen (graphics, cards, panels, captions, ink and screen recordings) must be easy to read on a phone. Check frames at phone size (about 640 px wide) before you apply, and make text larger when in doubt. *(owner, AgentUX video, 17 Oct 2026)*

## System design series
- Title: "System design for designers: <hook> (Part N)". The hook is what people remember (Part 1: "how I built my own Mobbin"), not only the product name. *(owner, AgentUX system design Part 1, 17 Oct 2026)*
- Thumbnail look: White frost background (the whole frame, with the table), the title on the left, the owner on the right in a frame where they smile and face the camera. Outline off. Nothing overlaps. *(owner, AgentUX system design Part 1, 17 Oct 2026)*
- Thumbnail text: tag DESIGN ENGINEERING; title "*SYSTEM DESIGN*" over "FOR DESIGNERS" with highlight (non-breaking spaces keep it on two lines); under it a picture at imageSize 1.6 with the hook on two lines. *(owner, AgentUX system design Part 1, 17 Oct 2026)*
- Thumbnail brands: show a brand's logo in place of its name (plain, no colour band), and at most three tool badges. Keep it clean and balanced: not every tool must show. *(owner, AgentUX system design Part 1, 17 Oct 2026)*
- Layouts: Speaker for the intro, stories and outro, with a 1.15x push-in; Screen for app tours; Ink for the hand-drawn YamDraw parts, the owner on the right. A layout changes only at a sentence start. *(owner, AgentUX system design Part 1, 17 Oct 2026)*
- Ink parts: zoom the sketch 1.5x, aimed where the most ink is, and change the aim only at a sentence start. Ink panel points are large (50 px) for phones. *(owner, AgentUX system design Part 1, 17 Oct 2026)*
- Cuts: tight cuts, then flow cuts for repeats, side stories and fillers. No speed-up. Aim for less than 12 minutes. *(owner, AgentUX system design Part 1, 17 Oct 2026)*
- Captions: upload them as an SRT file; do not burn them in. *(owner, AgentUX system design Part 1, 17 Oct 2026)*
- Description: what the project is, the parts drawn as a numbered list, why designers need system design, the links, the gear line and #SystemDesign #UXDesign #AINative. One chapter for each part. *(owner, AgentUX system design Part 1, 17 Oct 2026)*
- Graphics: mix look, accent #F5C42C. For phones, scale them up: name plate 1.5, feature list 1.35, title cards 1.27, link card 1.25, ink panels 1.2. Cards and panels stay out of the Ink parts. Logo badges use each brand's official logo. *(owner, AgentUX system design Part 1, 17 Oct 2026)*
