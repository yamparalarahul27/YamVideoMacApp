# The Story style: a study of three reels (plan)

**Status (2026-10-21):** study done. The owner picked three first parts (Page, Moves, Loop
end), one later part (One object) and three Style.md rules, now in Style.md (Shorts). Plan
only: no app code changes. Nothing is built yet.
**Source:** the owner, 21 Oct 2026: three Instagram reels they like "for their motion and
the way they tell a story". They want a new YamVideo style from them.

The reels (the owner's links):

1. R1: [instagram.com/reels/DelUSWyDQ1o](https://www.instagram.com/reels/DelUSWyDQ1o/)
   (varunmayya.words), 31.1 s. A founder explains how they spend money, with coins.
2. R2: [instagram.com/reel/DetwXzFjEtc](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DetwXzFjEtc/)
   (varunmayya.words), 35.8 s. A podcast clip about passion and discipline, with one chart.
3. R3: [instagram.com/reels/Def9pj5TWtA](https://www.instagram.com/reels/Def9pj5TWtA/)
   (tenfoldmarc), 27.3 s. A list of 17 video effects, with a demo of each one.

## How the study was made

- Each reel was opened read-only in the built-in browser, on Instagram's public embed page
  (the reel page asks for a sign-in; nobody signed in). No video was downloaded or saved.
- Frames were looked at every 0.25 to 0.75 s, and every 0.03 to 0.05 s at the main moves.
- The sound was not heard. A level trace (RMS and three bands: 30–150 Hz, 300–3000 Hz,
  5–12 kHz, every 50 ms) ran in the page with the output at zero. It shows where the voice
  stops, the floor under the voice, and short sounds outside the voice.
- No frame of these reels is in this repository. The pictures in the boards are new mocks.

## Study

### R1: coins (31.1 s, 720×1280)

**Hook (0.0–1.3 s).** A strong claim, in words, at once: sales gets too little credit. The
speaker is in a grey box on an orange wood backdrop with a halftone dot texture; their body
under the box takes the wood's colour. White words at the lower left: one big word, then two
small lines. No greeting, no context.

**Beats.**

| Time (s) | Beat | Picture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0–1.3 | Claim | Speaker full frame, words lower left |
| 1.25–1.5 | Change | The frame shrinks to a round-corner card (1.28), a squircle (1.39), a circle (1.45); the backdrop goes cream |
| 1.5–3.3 | The past: at 25 they ran companies the wrong way | Words top left; "25" big in orange. The round speaker drops on a time line (2.1) and flips into an orange coin with "25" on it (2.4) |
| 3.3–3.8 | Change | Whip: the page blurs, the coin spins |
| 3.75–7.0 | The old split: of 10 rupees, 8 to product, 2 to marketing | Ten coins drop into a 2×5 grid, one by one. Eight fly into a dark pile with "8" and a label; two make an orange pile with "2" |
| 7.3–10.2 | It took 13 years to learn | A coin on a ruler. A wide speaker card comes in (8.0), then moves down onto the ruler (9.0) |
| 10.3–15.3 | The new split: 8 to marketing, 2 to product | Dark page. A white UI card with an orange glow tilts in, in 3D: a budget and two columns. Coins fly into the columns; the counts tick up (6, 8); push-in on one column (12.5), then out; a box icon for the product (15.0) |
| 15.5–17.3 | How they do it | Cream page, wide speaker card in the top third |
| 17.5–19.0 | Give away part of the company | A dark coin becomes a pie; slices cut and fly |
| 19.0–22.3 | Good co-founders make 2 act like 10 | Dashed slots fill with coins. The pie slices fall onto three people and become their hats |
| 22.5–23.0 | Change | Whip pan, sideways |
| 23.5–25.5 | Never cut the marketing | Speaker card, orange coin stack under it |
| 25.75–27.5 | Marketing gives leads | The stack bursts into a network of coins and dots that grows |
| 28.0–29.3 | Leads give more | A road in perspective; coins roll down it at the camera |
| 29.5–31.1 | Punch line | One big orange coin with the last two words on it. Hold 1.6 s |

**Shots and cuts.** One camera, medium shot, seated. The speaker is on screen for about a
quarter of the time, always inside a shape (box, card, circle). The rest is flat motion
graphics. Changes come every 1–2 s.

**Text.** One sans (SF Pro or Inter type), semibold to bold, tight letter spacing.
Left-aligned block at the top left: x about 8 % of the width, top about 13 % of the height
(about x 86, y 250 on a 1080×1920 Short: inside YamVideo's clear area). Two sizes: lines
about 3.7 % of the height (about 72 px at 1920), the key word about twice that (about
140 px), in the accent. How it moves:

- The whole phrase comes in at once in a pale "ghost" grey, 20 px to the right of its place,
  and slides in over about 0.15 s.
- Each word turns to ink on the frame it is spoken, with a short blur (about 0.1 s).
- When the next phrase starts, the earlier lines dim to grey. At a new beat, the block blurs
  out in about 0.2 s and the next block starts at the top again.
- One key word per phrase (a number or the point): twice the size, in the accent colour.

**Moves and curves.** Shape morphs and whips take 0.2–0.4 s with a strong ease in and out
(expo type): slow, very fast, slow. Coins drop with a small bounce. Counters tick. A short
push-in shows a detail, then pulls back. Motion blur on every fast move.

**Colour.** Cream page (about #F4F1EA), ink (about #2A2A2A), one accent (burnt orange, about
#B4612C), pale grey for the ghost words. One dark scene (near black) with a white UI card
for the "today" part: the change of page colour marks the change of time.

**Sound (trace).** The voice is dry and in front. In pauses the level falls to about
−55 dBFS, so there is no clear music bed (if there is one, it is 30 dB or more under the
voice). Short high sounds outside the voice at 22.5 s, 26.0 s and 27.5 s: the whip, the
burst and the road (soft swishes). A low hit near 29.0 s, when the last coin lands. Then
1.6 s of full silence for the hold: the reel loops clean.

**Why it feels high-effort.** One object (the coin) carries the whole story and changes
form at each beat: speaker → coin → piles → pie → hats → network → road → the last coin.
Each number that is said has a picture. Nothing is decoration.

### R2: the cup (35.8 s, 720×1280)

**Hook (0.0–3.6 s).** A clear opinion, in words, from the first frame: do the work fully or
lose to the person who does. The page is off-white (about #EFEEEA). The words are centred
in the top third. The speaker is in a square card (about 86 % of the width, round corners
about 24 px at 720, a soft shadow) in the lower half.

**Beats.**

| Time (s) | Beat | Picture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0–3.6 | Opinion | Page layout: words top, speaker card bottom |
| 4.0–5.4 | Confession: they did half the work for years | Cut to the other person's face in the same card |
| 5.4–6.0 | Change | The card drops with motion blur, the words blur out, a dark page rises from the bottom with a soft edge |
| 6.0–15.5 | Proof: slow growth for six years, then fast growth | Dark page. An axis draws from the left; a half-full teal "cup" marks the first value; a line draws to the second; a teal bracket shows the span of years. The cup fills (14.75), and the line bends up steeply with a soft glow (15.5) |
| 16.0–19.0 | Why it changed | Back to the page layout |
| 19.25–29.0 | Thesis: passion feels like play; discipline alone feels like work | Page layout; three blocks change with a cross-blur (26.6, 29.0) |
| 29.75–31.0 | The turn | The disciplined person loses in the end |
| 31.25–34.0 | Close | Cut to the other person, then back |
| 34.25–35.8 | Loop end | The card drops away, the words move to the centre, and the teal cup becomes the full stop of the last line. Hold 1.5 s |

**Text.** The same ghost-then-ink reveal as R1, but centred. Lines about 3.4 % of the height
(about 66 px at 1920), key words about 7 % (about 130 px). Key words in teal (about #3F9CA3)
or in big ink.

**Colour.** Off-white page, ink, one teal accent, one dark scene for the data. The cup is
half full when they did half the work and full when they did all of it: the accent shape is the
idea.

**Sound (trace).** Voice in front, room tone or a very quiet bed near −55 dBFS in pauses.
One soft high tick near 11.0 s, when the bracket draws. After the last word (34.0 s), a low
tail fades out over about 1.3 s under the hold.

**Why it feels high-effort.** A plain podcast clip becomes a designed page: the words carry
the story, the card keeps the face clean, and one chart with one metaphor proves the point.

### R3: the list (27.3 s, 720×1280)

**Hook (0.0–2.0 s).** A number and a claim, with the proof already on screen: the title at
the top (the number in yellow), a numbered list of 17 items in two columns, and a phone-size
card between the speaker's hands that plays the first demo.

**Beats.**

| Time (s) | Beat | Picture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0–16.5 | Proof montage | 17 demos in the held card, about 1 s each. The counter (01/17) is on the card. The item in the list turns yellow and bold while its demo plays |
| 17.0–22.5 | Offer | The overlay goes away. Plain talking head; white bold lower-case captions of 1–3 words, centred at mid height |
| 22.75–27.3 | Call to action | The captions move to the top; the card shows a mock of their profile and comments; a keyword comment comes in; "Follow" changes to "Following" on the last frame |

**Text.** Bold sans, white, tight. The list items are small, on dark see-through plates with
round corners. The current item is the only yellow text.

**Sound (trace).** Voice only; pauses fall to −44 to −49 dBFS. No clear bed or hits.

**Why it feels high-effort.** Seventeen real demos in 16 s, each one tied to a line of the
list: the viewer sees the count go up and the proof at the same time.

## What the three have in common

1. **The claim comes first.** In the first 2 s the main point is on screen in words. No
   greeting, no context.
2. **The face is in a shape on a page.** A card or a circle on a flat page; the words sit on
   the page, not on the face.
3. **Words show the speech, word by word.** The phrase shows in ghost grey, each word inks in
   when it is said, one key word is big and in the accent, older lines dim.
4. **One accent colour, one font, two sizes.**
5. **One object carries the story** (coin, cup) and changes form at each beat. The speaker
   can become the object.
6. **A new picture every 1–2 s.** Moves are fast (0.2–0.4 s) with a strong ease and motion
   blur.
7. **A change of page colour marks a change of part** (cream → dark for proof or "today").
8. **A loop end.** The last words move to the centre and hold 1.5 s; the object becomes the
   full stop. The sound stops or fades.
9. **The voice leads the sound.** Little or no music; soft swishes only on big moves.

## What the owner picked (21 Oct 2026)

Six ideas were shown as animated phone-size mocks (new sample words, no frames of the reels).
The owner picked:

| Idea | Pick |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Page: the face in a card on a page, the words above | **Part 1** |
| 3. Shrink and moves: shape morphs, drop, whip, cross-blur | **Part 2** |
| 5. Loop end: the last words held 1.5 s, a dot as the full stop | **Part 3** |
| 4. One object: one dot or coin carries the story | **Part 4, later** |
| 2. Story text: ghost words ink in as they are said | Not picked |
| 6. Count list: a list that lights up with each proof clip | Not picked |

Key word colour on the cream page: **dark gold, about #A8781A** (a dark tone of the channel
yellow #F5C42C). The yellow itself has about 1.5:1 contrast on cream, too low. The dark gold
has about 3.5:1: enough for the big key word (large text needs 3:1), not for small text.

Style.md rules added after the owner's yes (section Shorts, Library revision 28):

- Claim first: the first 2 seconds show the main point in words on screen. No greeting and
  no context before it.
- One key word: use one accent colour, and at most one key word in a phrase, at twice the
  size.
- Loop end: each Short ends on its last words, held 1.5 s at the centre, so it loops.

## What the app has today

- **Shorts** (`Sources/Shorts.swift`): a 1080×1920 canvas, a 9:16 window of the recording,
  and `zoom` (0.5625–1): the picture zoomed out to a square at the top; its bottom edge
  fades into a blurred copy. `screen: top` puts a screen recording at the top. The clear
  area (`ShortFormat.clearArea`) is x 48–888, y 240–1520; YouTube's rail is right of x 888
  from y 960 down.
- **Short captions**: burned in by libass (Helvetica Bold, 60, 72 or 90 px, white with an
  outline), centred in the clear area. Under a graphic they step up, stay over, or hide.
- **Graphics**: `Resources/Graphics/graphics.js`, drawn the same in the preview and the
  HyperFrames export. Bundled fonts: Inter 400 and 700, Virgil. Cards glide in and blur out
  (PR #79). Kinetic text and section cards cover the whole picture.
- **Layouts per time** for long videos (`LayoutSpan`, with a bubble per span since PR #81).
  Shorts have one layout for their whole length.
- **Sound cues** (4.19) follow graphics in the long video. Shorts keep only the voice.

## Part 1: the Page layout for Shorts

**What it is.** A Short layout: a flat page with the picture in a card. The words sit on the
page above the card, so no text goes on the face (the owner's rule; PR #78).

- Page colour: cream (#F4F1EA, default) or dark (about #141716). Ink #2A2A2A on cream,
  white on dark.
- Card shape: **square** (840×840), **wide** (840×472, 16:9) or **round** (a circle, 480 px).
  Card place: **bottom** (default; the square is at y 680–1520), **top** or **middle**. The
  card is 840 px wide at x 48, so it stays in the clear area and left of YouTube's rail.
- Card corners 36 px and a soft shadow (y 24 px, blur 48 px, 18 % black). The window of the
  recording fills the card (the window's centre stays the face).
- The text area is the clear area above (or under) the card: on a square card at the
  bottom, y 240–640. Captions move into the text area, in ink with no outline on cream.
- Graphics with an accent use the dark gold on cream (owner's pick). On the dark page they
  use the channel yellow.

**What it needs in the app.**

1. Model: `Short.page: ShortPage?` (`colour`, `card`, `place`); nil is today's full frame.
   It replaces `zoom` while it is on. Project format bump.
2. Preview: a page layer, the card as a layer with corner radius and shadow.
3. Export: in the Short's ffmpeg graph, a colour source for the page, the window scaled into
   the card rect, a rounded-rect alpha mask, and a blurred, darkened mask for the shadow.
4. Captions: a page caption style (place: the text area; colour: ink or white; no outline).
5. Graphics clear of the face: in a page Short, the card is the face's box for
   `BubbleClear` (PR #78); graphics go in the text area.
6. Agents: `updateShort {page: {colour, card, place}}` (null: full frame). Catalog and
   Agents Manual 4.8. Inspector: a **Page** switch with **Card** and **Place** pop-ups (short
   labels).
7. Checks: the card stays in the clear area for each shape and place; the preview and the
   export match at one frame (frame parity); a phone-size check at 640 px wide.

## Part 2: Moves (shrink, drop, whip, blur)

**What it is.** A Short can change its page over time, with a fast move at each change, as
in the reels. A change starts only at a sentence start.

| Move | What it does | Time and curve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shrink** | Full frame → round-corner card → circle (the hook ends, the story starts) | 0.3 s; the card shape at 40 %; expo in-out |
| **Drop** | The card drops out with motion blur; a new page colour rises from the bottom with a soft edge (light → dark for proof, and back) | 0.35 s; expo in-out |
| **Whip** | The page pushes out sideways with motion blur; the next page pushes in | 0.2 s; expo in-out |
| **Blur** | The text block blurs out while the next block blurs in | 0.25 s; sine in-out |

The curve: about cubic-bezier(0.8, 0, 0.2, 1): slow, very fast, slow. Motion blur on
Drop and Whip, from the speed of each frame (a directional blur, or 5 sub-frames blended).

**What it needs in the app.**

1. Model: `Short.pages: [ShortPageSpan]` (`at`, `page` or nil for full frame, `move`), like
   `LayoutSpan` for long videos. The first span is the Short's start. Project format bump.
2. Preview and export of each move at the same times (the export draws the moves in its
   ffmpeg graph, or as a HyperFrames layer over the page; choose in the build).
3. Sound: Shorts must mix sound cues (today they keep only the voice). Cues `page.swish`
   (Drop, Whip) and `page.hit` (a low hit when a card lands), from the owner's sound
   library, at a level under the voice (4.19).
4. Agents: `setShortPages {shortID, pages}`; a change refused when it is not at a sentence
   start (read `transcript`). Manual 4.8.
5. Checks: each move's start and length in the export; the motion blur only during a move;
   the swish is under the voice.

## Part 3: Loop end

**What it is.** After the last word, the card drops away (Drop, 0.35 s), the last caption
moves to the centre of the page, a dot in the accent pops in as its full stop, and the
frame holds 1.5 s. The voice has ended; a music bed or a tail fades out over 1 s. The
viewer's loop starts again on the claim (Style.md, Shorts: Claim first).

**What it needs in the app.**

1. Model: `Short.loopEnd: Bool` (on by default for a page Short; Style.md, Shorts: Loop
   end). The Short gets 1.5 s more than its last word; its length stays 180 s or less.
2. Preview and export of the hold: the page, the centred words, the dot; no picture.
3. Agents: `updateShort {loopEnd}`. Manual 4.8.
4. Checks: the last 1.5 s has no voice; the words are centred in the clear area; the length
   rule holds.

## Part 4 (later): One object

**What it is.** Flat explainer scenes in the page colours (ink, page, one accent), where one
shape carries the story and changes form at each beat, as the coin in R1 and the cup in R2.
The speaker's circle (Part 2, Shrink) can hand its place to the first dot.

- `split`: N dots drop in (small bounce), then fly into groups with a count and a label
  (for example 10 hours: 8 to edits, 2 to ideas).
- `growth`: an axis draws, points show values, a bracket shows a span, and the last part of
  the line bends up. The marker fills from half to full.
- Later kinds, only if needed: `pie` (a part cut away), `network` (dots and lines grow).

**What it needs in the app.** New cover kinds in `graphics.js` with props and checks, sound
cue `dot.tap` (soft, one per drop), catalog and Agents Manual 4.5, a project format bump,
and frame parity checks. This part needs Parts 1 and 2 first.
