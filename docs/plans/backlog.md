## Thumbnails: the science of a click (added 2026-10-18)

Source: "Science Behind Thumbnails That Actually Work." by HimanshuG,
https://youtu.be/9_7q6vUaRsM (14:46). Its parts: the 0.5 s rule to win attention,
the information gap, the path of the viewer's eye, and YouTube's own way to get more
clicks (most likely Test & Compare, which tries up to three thumbnails).

Possible work for the thumbnail maker:
- Check each design at the size of a phone's home feed and search list, and say what
  does not read in 0.5 s.
- Help write a title and a thumbnail that open a question the title does not answer.
- Show where the eye goes first (face, then the biggest words), and warn when the
  important part is not there.
- Make three versions for YouTube's Test & Compare, and keep which one won.
